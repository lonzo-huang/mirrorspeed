## 本次更新
- 新增 **Google Play 应用内订阅**：Android 可直接在应用内用 Google Play 开通会员（月/季/半年/年），自动续订，可在 Google Play「订阅」里随时管理或取消。
- 订阅页面根据平台显示对应的计费与续订说明。

### 安装
**Android**：下载 APK 安装（需允许「未知来源」）；或在 Google Play 搜索「镜速加速器」安装。
**Windows**：下载 ZIP 解压后运行 mirrorspeed_vpn.exe（首次右键 → 以管理员身份运行）

## What's new
- Added **Google Play in-app subscriptions**: on Android you can now subscribe inside the app via Google Play (monthly / quarterly / half-year / yearly), auto-renewing, managed or cancelled anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions.
- The subscription screen now shows billing/renewal terms for the right store.

### Install
**Android**: download the APK and install (allow unknown sources), or get it from Google Play.
**Windows**: download the ZIP, extract and run mirrorspeed_vpn.exe (first launch: right-click → Run as administrator)
